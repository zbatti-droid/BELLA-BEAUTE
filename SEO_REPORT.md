# SEO & Accessibility Report

## SEO

- ✅ Semantic sections, title/meta baseline, robots and schema baseline exist.
- ⚠️ Sitemap currently contains hash-fragment URLs (`/#services`, etc.); use crawlable routes.
- ❌ No verified canonical URL or Arabic/French hreflang implementation.
- ⚠️ Local business schema, opening hours, phone and address need final real business data verification.
- ❌ No Search Console, Lighthouse SEO or structured-data validation run.

## Accessibility

- ✅ Many images have `alt`; interactive controls have several labels.
- ⚠️ Modal focus trap, focus return, Escape handling and inline form error announcements are missing.
- ⚠️ Keyboard navigation and contrast need manual WCAG testing.
- ⚠️ Heading hierarchy and language/RTL switching need screen-reader verification.
